# Work / Lab research notes

Research date: August 2026

The Work / Lab expansion is grounded in a simple operating observation: teams rarely lack information entirely; they lack a dependable path from fragmented inputs to an owned decision. The four business prototypes make that path concrete without presenting synthetic interactions as client results.

## Pain points translated into prototypes

- Reporting automation: manual stitching, late source refreshes, duplicate rows, missing categories, and unclear exception ownership.
- Revenue and margin forecasting: assumptions hidden in a spreadsheet, cash timing separated from margin, and collections risk treated as an afterthought.
- Pipeline intelligence: open value without health context, stale next steps, incomplete decision-maker fields, and forecasts that cannot explain their score.
- Support operations: backlog volume without issue pattern, SLA risk without routing context, repeat contact without a customer-story view, and drafts without a human approval checkpoint.

## Official market context

- [Intuit Enterprise Technology Benchmark, 2026](https://erp.intuit.com/blog/guide/enterprise-technology-benchmark-report/) — reports that 67% say data silos hinder decision-making.
- [QuickBooks Late Payments Report, 2026](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/small-business-late-payments-report-2026/) — reports that 59% of invoices are 30+ days overdue.

These are published market-context figures and source claims. They are not EliOra performance results, customer outcomes, or forecasts.
