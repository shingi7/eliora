    Forecast methodology

    Designed as a legible planning model: a fixed synthetic weekly revenue baseline is adjusted by demand and price, direct cost is applied to revenue, payroll is treated as a fixed operating load, and collection delay shifts cash timing and AR risk.

    • Inputs are bounded ranges paired with numeric fields.
    • Every chart value has a table equivalent.
    • Preset scenarios are deterministic and local to this page.
    • Use this to discuss workflow design—not to make financial decisions.
