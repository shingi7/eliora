How to read this lab
Start with the overview, then inspect the player and team surfaces. The charts preserve their original data contracts while the shell, palette, and decision framing are EliOra-specific.

Three pillars of value

The prototypes keep the evidence close to the decision: compare the profile, understand the style, and inspect the drivers.

Recruitment intelligence

Identify striker profiles and best-fit clubs with transparent, explainable metrics.

Tactical & style clarity

Reveal how a team plays, where strengths cluster, and which profiles match its style.

Explainable model support

Move from gut feel to evidence-backed decisions with interpretable drivers of points.

Why this matters commercially

Clubs face an overload of metrics and opinions. We reduce noise by translating complex data into clear, decision-grade insights—faster identification of fit, stronger recruitment alignment, and better tactical clarity for performance planning.
