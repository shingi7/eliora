Business Systems Lab

Current operational pain,
made tangible.

These prototypes make recurring operational pain visible: reporting that depends on manual stitching, forecasts that hide assumptions, pipeline reviews that lack next steps, and support queues that obscure the pattern behind the ticket.

They are not packaged products or client case studies. They are working surfaces for asking better questions about data models, workflow ownership, interface clarity, and the checks that make a decision dependable.
