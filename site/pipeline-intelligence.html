      Scoring model

      Explainable by design

      A synthetic health score combines stage movement, recency, next-step presence, decision-maker coverage, and support signals. It is a triage aid, not a prediction of outcome.

      Select a deal to inspect its inputs.

      Why this score?

      The synthetic health score is a weighted triage signal:

      • 30% next-step recency and completeness
      • 25% stage movement in the last 30 days
      • 20% decision-maker coverage
      • 15% account and segment fit fields
      • 10% support or delivery risk signals

      Inspectable rule: open the deal detail to see the fields and recommendation behind each local score.
